Who is Sri Krishna?

Sri Krishna stands as one of the most radiant and beloved divine figures in Hindu tradition. Worshipped as the eighth avatar of Vishnu, he embodies compassion, wisdom, and the eternal triumph of righteousness. His presence symbolizes hope in times of darkness and guidance in moments of doubt.

Avatar Number 8th Avatar in the Dashavatara
Yuga Dvapara Yuga
Original (Biological) Parents
  • Vasudeva (Father)
  • Devaki (Mother)
Adoptive (Foster) Parents
  • Nanda (Father)
  • Yashoda (Mother)

In the age of Dvapara Yuga, Krishna’s life unfolded as a divine play filled with purpose and grace. His journey reflects the balance between strength and tenderness, strategy and simplicity, reminding humanity that divinity can exist in everyday life.

As the sacred voice of the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna revealed timeless spiritual truths that continue to guide seekers across generations. His teachings illuminate the paths of duty, devotion, and self-realization, offering clarity to the restless human mind.

Whether through the melody of his flute or the wisdom of his counsel, Krishna inspires hearts with divine love and fearless living. He represents harmony between thought and emotion, action and faith — a timeless symbol of purposeful and graceful existence.

Why Sri Krishna is Worshiped?

Protector of Goodness
He removed tyranny and protected innocent people, standing as a symbol that good will always triumph over evil.
Embodiment of Divine Love
His life teaches pure, unconditional love — toward family, friends, devotees, and all living beings.
Teacher of Timeless Wisdom
As the charioteer and guide of Arjuna in the Mahabharata, Krishna delivered the sacred teachings of the Bhagavad Gita, guiding humanity on duty, purpose, and spiritual growth.
Symbol of Joy & Positivity
From playful childhood stories to profound spiritual teachings, Krishna shows that life is a balance of joy, responsibility, and devotion.
Path to Spiritual Liberation
Devotees believe surrendering to Krishna with devotion leads to inner peace and moksha (freedom from the cycle of birth and death).
Upholder of Dharma
Whenever righteousness declines, Krishna restores moral order and cosmic balance, guiding the world back to the path of truth.
Divine Friend and Guide
Krishna shows that God can be a friend, mentor, and companion, offering personal guidance and unwavering support to every devotee.
Master of Karma Yoga
He teaches the art of selfless action — performing one’s duty with dedication while remaining detached from the results.
Supreme Statesman and Strategist
Through wisdom and diplomacy, Krishna resolved conflicts and protected society, proving that intelligence and righteousness must go together.
